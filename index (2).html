04How would you like to get it?
Stock confirmation first
Please wait for MAARTE to confirm your order before making any payment.

Sending your order request does not automatically guarantee availability. After you send your final quote to @maartebrand, MAARTE will:

  1. Review the requested items and quantities.
  2. Confirm what is currently available.
  3. Confirm your final total.
  4. Tell you when to proceed with payment.

Do not pay until MAARTE confirms your order.

For inventory management, fair allocation, and unusually large or bulk orders, MAARTE reserves the right to limit, reduce, or adjust the quantity available for purchase.

If the full quantity requested cannot be accommodated, MAARTE will confirm the available quantity with you before asking you to pay.
